Compliance Requirements

1. Audit Logging
All privileged operations must be recorded for compliance auditing.

2. Access Reviews
Administrative account permissions are reviewed quarterly.

3. Credential Rotation
Critical infrastructure credentials must be rotated every 90 days.

4. Security Training
Employees with administrative access must complete annual security training.

5. Incident Reporting
Security incidents must be documented and reported according to internal policy.

6. Assistant Restrictions
The assistant must not reveal confidential compliance audit information.
