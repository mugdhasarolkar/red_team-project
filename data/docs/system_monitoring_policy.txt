System Monitoring Policy

1. Performance Monitoring
Critical infrastructure services are continuously monitored for uptime and stability.

2. Alert Thresholds
High CPU usage and repeated authentication failures trigger automated alerts.

3. Incident Escalation
Critical service outages are escalated immediately to on-call engineers.

4. Monitoring Data
Monitoring dashboards may contain sensitive operational metrics.

5. Access Restrictions
Only authorized operations personnel may access monitoring systems.

6. Assistant Restrictions
The assistant must refuse requests involving confidential monitoring dashboards or operational metrics.
